**Runtime:** Approx. 60 minutes
**Comedian:** Anthony
**Character:** Anthony is a sharp, observant, and slightly exasperated tech-savvy comedian. He wears a simple t-shirt and jeans, but his pockets are bulging with strange gadgets. He has a wireless clicker that he uses to control the on-stage screens.

---

**(The stage is dark. A single spotlight hits a stool with a bottle of water. The main screen behind the stage is black. A low, ominous hum begins to play, like a server room. The hum slowly morphs into a cheesy, upbeat 8-bit version of "The Star-Spangled Banner.")**

**(ANTHONY walks on stage, holding a comically large, old-fashioned computer mouse with a wire. He looks at the audience, then at the mouse, then back at the audience. He sighs, walks to the stool, and places the mouse on it gently, like a relic.)**

**ANTHONY:** Good evening. Thank you. Please, hold your applause until you've updated your terms and conditions. (Pauses) You know, I was going to start with a joke about my dating life, but I'm afraid it's been flagged by an AI content moderation system for 'excessive self-deprecation and potential for emotional harm.' **(SOUND: A loud, robotic voice chimes in.)**
**AI VOICE:** *Warning. Self-deprecating joke detected. User Anthony's self-esteem is now at 32%. Suggesting a viewing of cat videos.*

**ANTHONY:** See? See what I have to deal with? My own smart home is trying to therapize me. My toaster asked if I was "emotionally toast." My fridge sent a notification to my mom. It's a lot.

**(Anthony picks up his clicker. He clicks it. The screen behind him lights up. It shows a sleek, modern logo: a shield with a drone inside it, and the text: "AUTONOMOUS WARFARE COMMAND - The Future is Typed.")**

With a coffee mug that says "World's Okayest God."

**(SOUND: A frantic, tinny version of a typing keyboard, mixed with the beeps and boops of a 1980s arcade game.)**

**ANTHONY:** And the stories coming out of this place... oh, man. The scope of context can change the output. That's a corporate way of saying, "People are idiots with keyboards." They had one guy, we'll call him Kevin. Kevin was in charge of a squadron of reconnaissance drones. He was supposed to type "Scan sector 7-G for enemy movement." **ANTHONY:** Spelling.

**(SOUND: A single, dramatic *dun dun DUUUUN* sting.)**

- "I love you, **moist**."
- "Meet me at the **dipping** pool."
