# **THE NEW WHITE HOUSE**

## **A Vision for an Expanded Presidential, Diplomatic, International Conference and Hospitality Complex**

*An imaginative architectural proposal honoring the presidential era of Donald J. Trump, combining American architectural traditions, international diplomacy, advanced communications, hospitality, and monumental public gathering spaces.*

The vision is to create a more comprehensive presidential complex, in which the private residential mansion, diplomatic reception facilities, international hotel accommodations, national conference halls, theatres, and technological infrastructure are distinct architectural environments.

The most important principle is separation of purpose. The presidential family should have its own private residential quarters. Visiting heads of state should have elegant accommodations. International delegations should have dedicated diplomatic meeting facilities. Large international conferences should operate independently of the household.

This would make the White House conceptually more like a sophisticated presidential campus than a single mansion.

The dimensions and room counts below are hypothetical planning targets, not descriptions of existing White House facilities or authorized construction plans.

Each curved table would have localized speakers, allowing delegates to hear the presentation clearly without excessive room volume.

The system could support:

- Original-language audio.
- Simultaneous interpretation.
- Individual headphone listening.
- Multilingual video conferencing.
- Directional loudspeaker coverage.
- Live transcription.
- Archival recording.
- Remote participation.

The objective would be exceptional intelligibility, not simply loudness.

5. Internal service distribution.
