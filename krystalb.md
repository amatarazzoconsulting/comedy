# **KRYSTAL: LITTLE BURGERS, BIG PERSONALITIES**

## **ACT 1 – WELCOME TO THE ANIMAL KINGDOM**

ANTHONY: Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to Krystal, where the burgers are tiny, the excitement is enormous, and apparently the employees escaped from a very friendly zoo!

ANTHONY: I walked in expecting a hamburger. I walked out wondering if I had accidentally joined a wildlife documentary!

RAJ THE TIGER: Welcome to Krystal! I am Raj, your manager. We take pride in our service, teamwork, and our very unusual employee uniforms.

ANTHONY: Raj, why are you dressed like a tiger?

RAJ THE TIGER: Because management wanted someone who could lead with confidence, courage, and a little growl.

ANTHONY: Well, you certainly have the stripes for upper management!

## **ACT 2 – ANIMAL EMPLOYEE TRAINING CLASS**

INSTRUCTOR: Welcome, everyone, to Krystal's Animal Employee Training Academy! Today we will learn how each animal behaves, communicates, and performs its special duties. Remember, these are theatrical characters, and every animal has a role in making our restaurant experience unforgettable!

INSTRUCTOR: First, the mouse! The mouse is small, quick, alert, and very curious. At Krystal, the mouse works at the customer register. Mouse employees use quick hand movements, energetic expressions, and cheerful squeaks to welcome customers. When someone approaches the counter, the mouse stands tall, wiggles its nose, and says, "Squeak! Welcome to Krystal!"

MILO THE MOUSE: Squeak, squeak! I take orders, answer questions, and make sure every customer feels welcome!

INSTRUCTOR: Excellent, Milo! Next, the squirrel! Squirrels are energetic, fast-moving, and always busy collecting things. Our squirrel works at the second register and helps organize orders. The squirrel may twitch its nose, flick its tail, and make excited chattering sounds. Its favorite phrase is, "Chitter, chitter! Let's get that order moving!"

SAMMY THE SQUIRREL: I am ready! I can take an order, count fifty burgers, and still remember where I hid my acorns!

INSTRUCTOR: Next, the pig! The pig is enthusiastic, hardworking, and surprisingly talented at keeping things clean. Our pig handles cleaning duties, wiping tables, sweeping floors, and keeping the restaurant sanitary. The pig makes cheerful snorting and oinking sounds while working.

PORKY THE PIG: Oink! Oink! A clean restaurant is a happy restaurant!

INSTRUCTOR: Now, the raccoon! Raccoons are clever, curious, and excellent at investigating things. At Krystal, the raccoon works in packaging. The raccoon checks bags, organizes napkins, counts burgers, and makes sure every order is complete. Raccoons use little paw movements, suspicious glances, and excited chattering.

ROCKY THE RACCOON: Chatter, chatter! I inspect every bag. Nothing gets past my paws!

INSTRUCTOR: Next, the hamster! Hamsters are energetic little creatures known for running on wheels and storing food. Our hamster is the star of the bun-production department. The hamster performs a playful wheel-running routine while the bakery team prepares the buns. Remember, the wheel is theatrical entertainment, not actual food production!

HAMMY THE HAMSTER: Squeak! I'm running the bakery, and the bakery is running me!

INSTRUCTOR: Finally, our manager, the tiger! The tiger represents confidence, leadership, and teamwork. Raj supervises the restaurant, encourages employees, and keeps the entire animal kingdom working together. His signature sound is a friendly roar!

RAJ THE TIGER: ROAR! Teamwork makes the dream work!

INSTRUCTOR: And what about the animals customers rarely see? The owl watches over nighttime security, the beaver handles maintenance, the penguin looks after refrigeration, and the fox develops clever corporate strategies. Every animal has a responsibility!

ANTHONY: So let me understand this. The mouse takes the order, the squirrel counts the burgers, the pig cleans the floor, the raccoon checks the bag, and the hamster runs in circles?

INSTRUCTOR: Correct!

ANTHONY: That's the most organized zoo I've ever seen. And somehow, it's also a restaurant!

## **ACT 3 – MEET THE EMPLOYEES**

ANTHONY: At the register, we have two very special employees. Please welcome Milo the Mouse and Sammy the Squirrel!

MILO THE MOUSE: Squeak! Welcome to Krystal! May I take your order?

ANTHONY: Do you sell hamburgers or cheese?

MILO THE MOUSE: Both! But please don't ask me to carry the cheese. I have a reputation to maintain.

SAMMY THE SQUIRREL: Hello! What can I get you today?

ANTHONY: Fifty Krystals, please.

SAMMY THE SQUIRREL: Fifty? Oh, my goodness! That's a whole forest of burgers!

ANTHONY: Don't panic. Just put them in a bag.

SAMMY THE SQUIRREL: A bag? We have a special delivery system. We call it the squirrel strategy: collect everything, hide half of it, and forget where you put the rest!

ANTHONY: That's not a strategy. That's how my relatives organize Thanksgiving!

## **ACT 4 – THE CASH REGISTER**

MILO THE MOUSE: Would you like cheese on your Krystal?

ANTHONY: Absolutely.

MILO THE MOUSE: Would you like onions?

ANTHONY: Yes.

MILO THE MOUSE: Pickles?

ANTHONY: Yes.

MILO THE MOUSE: Would you like a tiny little hat for your burger?

ANTHONY: A hat?

MILO THE MOUSE: We have a very fashionable burger collection.

ANTHONY: I came for lunch, not a fashion show!

SAMMY THE SQUIRREL: Your total is ready!

ANTHONY: How much?

SAMMY THE SQUIRREL: Fifty burgers, fifty smiles, and one very exhausted squirrel!

ANTHONY: Do you take credit cards?

SAMMY THE SQUIRREL: Of course! We also accept acorns, but management says we have to stop accepting pinecones.

## **ACT 5 – THE HAMSTER BAKERY**

ANTHONY: Now, let's visit the bakery. I hear there's a hamster producing the buns.

HAMMY THE HAMSTER: Welcome to the bakery! I run the bun-production department!

ANTHONY: You mean you actually make the buns?

HAMMY THE HAMSTER: Absolutely! I run on a wheel, and the wheel runs the bakery!

ANTHONY: That's incredible. How many buns can you make in an hour?

HAMMY THE HAMSTER: Depends on the music!

ANTHONY: What happens when the music stops?

HAMMY THE HAMSTER: Production stops. I demand a snack break!

RAJ THE TIGER: Hammy, we need more buns!

HAMMY THE HAMSTER: Then somebody better turn up the music!

## **ACT 6 – THE PACKAGING DEPARTMENT**

ANTHONY: Now we meet Rocky the Raccoon, head of packaging and distribution.

ROCKY THE RACCOON: Welcome! Every Krystal is carefully packed, inspected, and delivered with pride.

ANTHONY: Why are you holding the bag up to the light?

ROCKY THE RACCOON: Quality control.

ANTHONY: What are you looking for?

ROCKY THE RACCOON: Missing fries, extra napkins, suspicious crumbs, and evidence of unauthorized cheese.

ANTHONY: Unauthorized cheese?

ROCKY THE RACCOON: We have a very strict cheese policy.

ANTHONY: Rocky, I ordered twenty burgers. Where are they?

ROCKY THE RACCOON: They're in the bag.

ANTHONY: I see three.

ROCKY THE RACCOON: The other seventeen are under investigation.

ANTHONY: This isn't a restaurant. It's a burger distribution mystery!

## **ACT 7 – THE CLEANING CREW**

ANTHONY: And now, please welcome Porky Pig, our cleaning specialist!

PORKY THE PIG: Oink! I keep this restaurant spotless!

ANTHONY: How do you clean the floor?

PORKY THE PIG: With a mop, disinfectant, and a little enthusiasm!

ANTHONY: Do you make animal noises while you clean?

PORKY THE PIG: Only when the floor is really dirty!

ANTHONY: That's dedication. Most employees just complain about the mess.

PORKY THE PIG: Oink! Clean floors, happy customers!

SAMMY THE SQUIRREL: I dropped an acorn!

PORKY THE PIG: Not again!

## **ACT 8 – THE SECRET ANIMAL STAFF**

ANTHONY: I heard there are other animals working here that customers never get to see.

RAJ THE TIGER: We have a very talented behind-the-scenes team.

ANTHONY: Who works at night?

OLIVER THE OWL: Hoo! I handle night security. Nobody sneaks into this restaurant without me noticing.

ANTHONY: What do you do all night?

OLIVER THE OWL: Watch, listen, and occasionally blink.

ANTHONY: Who fixes the building?

BENNY THE BEAVER: I handle maintenance. If something breaks, I build it back better!

ANTHONY: What about the refrigerators?

PERCY THE PENGUIN: That's my department. I keep everything cool.

ANTHONY: And who handles corporate strategy?

FELIX THE FOX: I do. I develop clever plans to increase sales, improve efficiency, and keep the squirrels from hiding the inventory.

SAMMY THE SQUIRREL: I object!

FELIX THE FOX: Objection noted. Acorn inventory will be audited.

## **ACT 9 – THE CORPORATE MEETING**

RAJ THE TIGER: Team, we have an important objective. We want to serve more customers, improve our packaging, and make every visit memorable.

ANTHONY: Raj, what's the corporate strategy?

RAJ THE TIGER: Bigger smiles, faster service, better burgers!

HAMMY THE HAMSTER: More music!

ROCKY THE RACCOON: Better bags!

PORKY THE PIG: Cleaner floors!

MILO THE MOUSE: More cheese!

SAMMY THE SQUIRREL: More acorns!

RAJ THE TIGER: No acorns in the food budget!

ANTHONY: That's the first responsible financial decision I've heard all day!

## **ACT 10 – THE FIFTY-BURGER CHALLENGE**

CUSTOMER: I'd like fifty Krystals, please.

MILO THE MOUSE: Fifty? Squeak! We have a big order!

SAMMY THE SQUIRREL: Everybody to your stations!

HAMMY THE HAMSTER: Starting bun production!

ROCKY THE RACCOON: Packaging department ready!

PORKY THE PIG: Floors are clear!

RAJ THE TIGER: Team, let's make this happen!

ANTHONY: Look at this! It's like the Olympics, except everybody is competing for hamburgers!

HAMMY THE HAMSTER: The buns are ready!

ROCKY THE RACCOON: The bags are ready!

SAMMY THE SQUIRREL: The order is ready!

ANTHONY: Fifty burgers in record time! Somebody give these animals a raise!

RAJ THE TIGER: Excellent work, team!

ANTHONY: Raj, where are the burgers?

ROCKY THE RACCOON: They're in the bags.

ANTHONY: Where are the bags?

SAMMY THE SQUIRREL: I may have hidden them.

## **ACT 11 – ANTHONY'S STAND-UP FINALE**

ANTHONY: I love this restaurant. It's the only place where the mouse takes your order, the squirrel handles your money, the pig cleans the floor, and the raccoon investigates your hamburger bag!

ANTHONY: The hamster makes the buns, the owl watches the building, the beaver fixes the plumbing, and the penguin keeps the refrigerator cold!

ANTHONY: And the manager is a tiger! That's not a management structure. That's an entire nature documentary with a cash register!

RAJ THE TIGER: We are a family here!

ANTHONY: Absolutely! A family where everybody has a job, everybody wears a costume, and nobody knows where the acorns went!

## **GRAND FINALE – THE KRYSTAL PARADE**

RAJ THE TIGER: Ladies and gentlemen, thank you for visiting Krystal!

MILO THE MOUSE: Squeak! Come back soon!

SAMMY THE SQUIRREL: Bring your friends!

PORKY THE PIG: We'll keep everything clean!

ROCKY THE RACCOON: We'll package every order with care!

HAMMY THE HAMSTER: And we'll keep those buns coming!

ANTHONY: Remember, folks, at Krystal the burgers may be little, but the personalities are enormous! Thank you, good night, and somebody please check the squirrel's pockets for the missing inventory!

ALL EMPLOYEES: LITTLE BURGERS! BIG EXCITEMENT! OINK! SQUEAK! HOO! ROAR!
