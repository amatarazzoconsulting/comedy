## **The premise**

What happens when a perfectly ordinary seafood restaurant decides that its employees should dress, speak, and behave like pirates?

Not just on Halloween. Not just for a promotional weekend. Every day.

The cashier becomes a first-class member of the Captain's crew. The manager becomes Admiral of the Cash Register. The fry cook becomes Master of the Deep Fryer. The dining room becomes a maritime entertainment zone.

And the customer?

The customer is no longer simply ordering fish.

The customer is boarding a vessel, accepting the risks of maritime commerce, and negotiating the price of a seafood platter with a woman wearing an eye patch.

Anthony introduces the idea after visiting Captain D's, where he encounters a beautiful clean tile floor, a well-presented fish menu, and a cashier who is learning the advanced capabilities of the register.

He asks her:

"Would you come into work dressed as a pirate?"

She says she would enjoy it.

Anthony immediately recognizes the problem.

**He's just discovered a business opportunity disguised as a ridiculous question.**

The comedy is affectionate. The employees are enthusiastic, the manager is trying to maintain operational standards, and the pirates are gradually discovering that the hardest thing about being a pirate is not walking the plank.

It's remembering how to void an order while maintaining an authentic maritime accent.

**Performance direction for Anthony:** Play the Captain as a confident entrepreneur who believes every ridiculous idea is a serious business opportunity. Your comedy comes from the contrast between your completely straight-faced business reasoning and the increasingly absurd behavior of the employees.

The manager should be the grounded character. The more seriously the manager takes restaurant operations, the funnier the pirates become.

The cashier should be the audience's favorite character: enthusiastic, expressive, and increasingly fluent in pirate speech.

**The central joke of the entire production:**

Captain D's has spent years establishing a maritime identity through its name.

Anthony is simply suggesting that the employees finally start acting like they work there.

And somewhere behind the cash register, a perfectly ordinary seafood transaction is about to become a voyage of extraordinary proportions.
